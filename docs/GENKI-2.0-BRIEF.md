# 1. WHAT GENKI IS

Genki is a Bulgarian managed workplace food and wellness benefit.

It should **not** feel like a vending-machine company.

The smart cooler is infrastructure.

**Genki is the service.**

The company provides:

- curated Bulgarian food and drinks;
- smart self-service access;
- restocking;
- service and operational management;
- assortment optimization;
- employer benefit options;
- Product Price Support;
- employee participation;
- social impact;
- monthly donation commitment.

The product philosophy is:

**Better food belongs at work.**

Core principles:

1. Products are carefully selected under the **Genki Product Standard**.
2. Genki works with **Bulgarian producers**.
3. Every month Genki donates **10% of its real profit after all costs**.
4. Genki should require very little recurring operational effort from the client.
5. Genki becomes better over time based on real usage.

## Product Price Support

Employer contributes toward product prices.

Possible support levels:

- 10%
- 20%
- 30%
- 50%
- 100% exists commercially but should **not** be automatically recommended online.

With basic Genki / Standard:

**€49/month/location management fee + actual employer subsidy.**

With Genki Benefit:

Price Support management is included.

Product Price Support does **not** reduce Genki shelf revenue. It changes who pays.

Employee pays the remainder.
